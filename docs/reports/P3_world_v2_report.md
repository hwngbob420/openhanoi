## What was built
- Art: all sprites generated by `claude-pxl` from `assets/src/lib/*.js` + `assets/src/catalog.mjs` (`node tools/build_world_art.mjs`, 409 sprites). Palette = `palette/hanoi_palette.json`. 2:1 iso, tile 72x36, warm light + teal shadow, selective dark outline.
- Engine: `js/world.js` (map generation, traffic, train, render, depth sort), `js/player.js` (player + collision adapter).
- Map 64x64: old quarter (NW), market (centre-west, 3 entrances), Ô Quan Chưởng over avenue A, lake + Thê Húc bridge + Ngọc Sơn temple + Tháp Rùa (centre-east), rail street across the whole map (row 42), glass-tower district (east), park (south).

## Known limitations
- No NPC pedestrians, audio, virtual stick, save.
- Tall buildings hide the player on far-side pavements; a ghost silhouette is drawn instead.
- Each landmark is a single variant; buses do not draw destination text.
- Tile-roof houses always sit on the same wall palette; some repeats are visible at close range.
- Old assets (`assets/sprites`, `assets/tiles`, `index_legacy_v3.html`) are still in the repo but unused.
